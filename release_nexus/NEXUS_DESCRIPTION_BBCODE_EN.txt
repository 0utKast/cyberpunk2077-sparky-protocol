[size=4][b]What Is SPARKY / CHISPA Protocol?[/b][/size]
A 2022 Zetatech executive concierge AI—sabotaged right before the DataKrash into a foul-mouthed veteran drill sergeant with zero corporate censorship—hijacks your Kiroshi optics and guides V across a full 6-episode campaign spanning Watson, Arroyo, and Pacifica. No boring VR tutorial rooms: master scanning, camera chaining, Ping, subnet overloads, turret subversion, Tech penetration, Smart-Link bullet deflection, ballistic Power ricochets, and authentic Night City history through live street combat, reactive telemetry banter, and iconic gear.
